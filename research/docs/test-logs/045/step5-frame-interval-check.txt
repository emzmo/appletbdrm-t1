## ADR proposal (interlock finding)
- ADR-049 (proposed): oracle masking during tbard validation/migration runs.
  `systemctl stop` is insufficient — the oracle's Restart= policy (static unit)
  self-restarts it, producing panic-retry loops against the busy card. For
  validation: use `systemctl mask` or `systemctl stop` + verify no auto-restart
  within +60s before starting tbard. For migration: the oracle's unit must be
  masked/disabled-by-policy before tbard takes over, or the Restart= policy
  must be removed. Coordinate with the interlock design (ADR-019).
